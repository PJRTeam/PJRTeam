The problems we get called about

Most clients don't call us because they want "digital governance." They call because one of these just happened:

  • "A former employee still has our Facebook login."

    Nobody knows who owns the page, the email on the account left two years ago, and now you can't post — or worse, they still can.

  • "We got hit with bad reviews and nobody responded."

    Families read every review before choosing a facility. Thirty days of silence reads as guilt.

  • "Our website doesn't match who we are anymore."

    You've rebranded, opened a new facility, or grown — but your site still looks like 2019, and referral sources notice.

  • "We have no idea what our last agency actually set up."

    Domains, hosting, ad accounts, page admin rights — scattered across personal emails and ex-vendors.

If any of these sound familiar, you don't have a marketing problem. You have an ownership problem. That's what we fix first.

Who we are

The team you call before it becomes a problem.

PJR Team is a healthcare-focused digital partner. We don't just post content or build websites — we make sure your organization actually owns and controls its digital presence: every login, every page, every domain, every review channel.

With over 12 years in senior living digital marketing and 20+ years in marketing overall, we know the realities you operate in: compliance sensitivity, family-facing reputation stakes, lean internal teams, and leadership that needs answers — not jargon. We partner with single organizations and multi-state brands scaling across affiliated facilities.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you only work with healthcare organizations?

Healthcare is our specialty — it's where governance and reputation stakes are highest. But our approach works for any organization that needs to control its digital presence, and we serve clients across industries.

We already have a marketing person/agency. Why would we need you?

We're not a replacement for marketing — we're the layer underneath it: ownership, access, governance, and risk. Many clients keep their creative resources and use us to make sure the foundation is secure.

What does an engagement look like?

Most clients start with a digital risk check, then move to a flat monthly program covering governance, social, reputation, and support. No long lock-ins, no surprise fees.

How fast do you respond to reviews?

Our standard is 24–48 hours for any public review, with escalation to you for anything sensitive.
