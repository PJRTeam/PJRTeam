Where census goes missing

Two leaks. Neither shows up in your CRM.

A family that rules you out online never calls. A nurse who quits never files a census report. That's why both leaks get missed.

Leak #1: The front door

Your Google profile, reviews and website are the first tour. Most families take it before they ever pick up the phone.

    of patients check online reviews before choosing a provider.

Leak #2: The back hallway

Morale shows up in turnover. Turnover shows up in quality scores, reviews and how many admissions you can safely accept.
