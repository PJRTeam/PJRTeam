The Digital Media Governance Standard for Skilled Nursing & Senior Living

Your staff carry phones into resident rooms. Your policy manual was written before that was true. Version 1.0 is the training and documentation standard that closes the gap.

Request the Standard Schedule a Facility Training

The problem, stated plainly

Since 2016, surveyors have been directed to treat demeaning photographs and recordings of residents as potential abuse. That is not a marketing citation. It follows the same path as any other abuse allegation — reporting timelines, investigation, potential immediate jeopardy.

Most facilities have a social media policy. Almost none have a training record they could hand a surveyor.

What the Standard covers

Three tiers, because exposure is not the same at every level.

  1. Frontline — 45 minutes

    Every staff member who carries a phone into the building. The Background Scan, consent, personal accounts, and what to do in the first hour after a bad post.

  2. Facility Leadership — 90 minutes

    Account ownership and access, the consent program, responding to reviews without disclosing PHI, incident documentation, and the approval chain.

  3. Corporate & Multi-Facility Marketing — half day

    Portfolio account governance, rollout and training records at scale, vendor contracts, and the crisis playbook.

What the facility keeps

Consent forms. Revocation log. Background Scan card for the breakroom. Access inventory. Offboarding checklist. Review response decision tree. Incident report form. Training attestation log. Drop-in policy language.

The training is the delivery mechanism. The documentation is the asset.

Why quarterly

Compliance training is annual because the regulations move slowly. Platforms do not. Google changes profile rules, Meta changes admin permissions, and a policy written last August is quietly wrong by March.

Standard subscribers receive a quarterly bulletin and an updated toolkit. The Standard is versioned and dated, so a facility can state exactly which version it trained against.

Who built it

PJR Team has spent roughly eight years inside skilled nursing digital operations — profile ownership, HIPAA-compliant social media, review governance, and staff training. Published in Provider Magazine (AHCA/NCAL) and McKnight’s Long-Term Care News.

Formats

On-site single facility · On-site portfolio rollout · Virtual by tier · Train-the-trainer license · Annual Standard subscription
