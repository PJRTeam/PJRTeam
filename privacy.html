Privacy

Privacy Policy

We reply within one business day and never share your information.

What we collect

When you use a form on pjrteam.com, we collect the details you submit so we can respond. That typically includes your name, email, organization or facility, and any message you choose to send. Census-check requests may also include your role, state, licensed bed count, and phone number.

How we use it

We use this information to reply to your inquiry, prepare a census check or risk check when requested, and follow up about PJR Team services. Submissions are routed to ContactUs@pjrteam.com.

We do not sell your information

We never share your information with third parties for their marketing. Form submissions are processed by our form provider so they can reach our inbox.

Questions
